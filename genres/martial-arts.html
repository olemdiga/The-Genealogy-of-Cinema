Martial Arts Cinema

The disciplined body as political text: resistance, honor, and the aesthetics of violence.

Martial arts films as sites of cultural memory and corporeal philosophy. From Bruce Lee's decolonizing fists to the balletic wuxia of Crouching Tiger, each film negotiates power, identity, and the limits of the human body. Analyzed through lenses of Foucault's discipline, Fanon's anti-colonial struggle, Nietzsche's will to power, and Mbembe's necropolitics.
