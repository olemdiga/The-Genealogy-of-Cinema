Assassin Cinema

The contract killer as existential figure: violence as labor, solitude as condition, ethics under erasure.

Assassin films explore the liminal space between life and death, where the professional killer becomes a vessel for examining sovereignty, biopolitics, and the value of human existence. Through lenses of Foucault's biopower, Mbembe's necropolitics, Nietzsche's nihilism, and Said's Orientalist gaze, each film interrogates the moral economy of murder-for-hire.
