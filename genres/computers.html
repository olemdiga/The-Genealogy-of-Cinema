Computer Cinema

Code as power: digital labor, surveillance capitalism, and the algorithmic condition.

Films about computing and its architects interrogate the structures of the information age. From the mythology of tech founders to the invisible architectures of social control, each film reveals how code shapes subjectivity, labor, and power. Analyzed through lenses of Foucault's discipline, Marx's immaterial labor, Baudrillard's simulation, and critical perspectives on platform capitalism.
