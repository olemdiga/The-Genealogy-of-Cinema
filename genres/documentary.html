Documentary Cinema

Reality refracted through critical lenses: decolonial thought, biopolitics, and the politics of representation.

Documentary as a site of truth-claims and power—each film interrogates the construction of reality, memory, and social struggle. Analyzed through the frameworks of Fanon, Mbembe, Foucault, Freire, and other thinkers who challenge the boundaries between observer and observed.
